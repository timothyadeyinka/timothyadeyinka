About Our Hotel

How We Begun

In one of the largest cities of Montana called Billings, which is one of the most beautiful and picturesque places in Montana, Helena, we chose to build a lovely high quality hotel which will give our guests the opportunity to experience a holiday with wonderful surroundings of nature and at the same time luxury and coziness.
Bourbon Bliss Hotel & Spa is a family-friendly hotel that offers a wide range of accommodation types, from rooms to suites. All this in the peaceful surroundings of our beautiful gardens, will make your time at Billings, Montana an unforgettable holiday.

Great Location

In the heart of Billings, Bourbon Bliss Hotel & Spa offers a comfortable stay in an elegant 19th century architecture. The hotel is close to Pictograph Cave State Park and ZooMontana. Location wise, Moss Mansion and ZooMontana are within walking distance. Stay at our Bourbon Bliss Hotel & Spa and experience a special sojourn with quintessential American charm.
