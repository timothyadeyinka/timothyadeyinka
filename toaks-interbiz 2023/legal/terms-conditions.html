Quality Policy

Through collective mind set, process innovation and continuous improvement, Toaks Interbiz Unique Enterprise exercise to always meet and exceed our customers' expectations in providing affordable products at the highest quality while keeping the consumer's needs in mind.

Quality is critical to our success: it is integral to and underpins all of our processes. We manage our activities and associated resources as a series of planned processes to produce the right assembly/queue, at the right time with minimum wastage while keeping the environment clean and purified from industrial chemicals.

Our individual processes are stuctured into a documented quality management system, known as which meets the requirements for ISO 9001:2015. We are commited to the continuous improvement of the products and services we provide, whilst also driving forward the effectiveness of our QMS (Quality Management System).

Our management team drives compliance with all statutory, regulatory, legislative and contractual requirements - and we provide an internal environment in which our people are fully involved in achieving our Quality Objectives.



Furthermore, to meet our objectives, we shall:

  • Provide sufficient resources and equipment to ensure that we can operate to the Quality Management System's standard of ISO 9001:2015,

  • Forge partnerships with our suppliers, interested parties and major players in the Private and Public Sectors to ensure our optimum business performance. We shall also ensure that our suppliers and partners that may be used in the delivery of our services also comply with our quality philosophy and company policies. Including the careful selection of suppliers.
